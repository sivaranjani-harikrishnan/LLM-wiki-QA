# Audience Guide: Sales / Onboarding

**Summary**: How to explain HeySales features and value to prospects and new customers.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What is HeySales?

HeySales is an AI-powered sales readiness platform built on top of Paperflite. It helps sales organisations:
- Train reps through AI-generated learning content (courses, podcasts, simulations)
- Assess reps through scored role-play calls (simulations) and real call scoring
- Track rep progress with detailed reports and analytics

## Key selling points by use case

### "We want to train reps consistently"
- **Courses**: Build structured multi-module programs combining documents, videos, quizzes, podcasts, and simulations
- **Sections**: Organise courses with sections (unit groups) for cleaner learner experiences
- **Skills tagging**: Tag every piece of content with skills for better discoverability

### "We want reps to practice sales calls"
- **Simulations**: AI plays the prospect; rep takes a Discovery, Cold Call, or Follow-up call
- **Elevator Pitch**: Rep delivers a timed monologue (30 sec to 2 min); no AI prospect
- **Profile Extraction**: Upload a real call recording to auto-generate a realistic AI prospect profile

### "We want to assess real sales calls automatically"
- **Real Call Scoring**: Connect Zoom + HubSpot or MS Dynamics → every Zoom call with a prospect (active deal) is automatically scored and pushed to the CRM as a note
- **Call Library**: Browse all past scored calls in one place with metadata and recordings

### "We want reps to learn at their own pace"
- **Podcasts**: AI-generated audio (conversational, monologue, or narrative style) from any documents or URLs; reps listen on web or mobile
- **Let's Learn (Microlearning)**: Reps generate private AI articles and podcasts from any content they choose

### "We want to track who's done what"
- **Reports**: Per-feature dashboards for completion rates, scores, time spent, rankings, and learner-level breakdowns
- **Learner List**: In-progress and completed columns per learning type with detailed timing data
- **Due dates**: Set deadlines; automated reminder emails on a cascading schedule

## Key facts for demos

- HeySales is accessed from the Paperflite left panel
- Must be enabled per account
- Roles: Admin, Content Publisher (create/manage), Normal User (learn), BI Analyst (reports only)
- Mobile app (iOS + Android) supports courses, podcasts, and My Space; simulations web-only
- All AI content generated in English (international docs supported)
