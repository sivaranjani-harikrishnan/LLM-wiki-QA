## Note on source document

`raw/d480b950-b052-4053-8bb0-71f9c0dda5bb_Live_call_help.pdf` is a design brief and competitive research document. It covers:
- Five core in-call problems (knowledge recall, discovery coaching, objection handling, buying signal detection, script compliance)
- Two audio capture approaches (bot-joined vs browser/desktop capture) with latency and quality trade-offs
- Types of guidance (informational, prescriptive, passive)
- UX patterns (floating panel, inline highlights, nudges, cue cards)
- Risk and failure modes
- Competitor references (Vivun, Poised, Colibri, Balto AI, Aside AI, Cluely AI)

It does not contain GIVEN/WHEN/THEN acceptance criteria. When formal ACs are produced, they should be added here.

The feature concept and design intent are documented in [[overview]].
