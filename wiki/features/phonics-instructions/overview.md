# Feature: Phonics Instructions

**Summary**: Allows users to define custom phonetic spellings for words so that those words are consistently pronounced correctly in all podcasts.

**Sources**: `raw/38c38369-082f-47e8-865c-be38ac9ae87d_Phonics_Instructions.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it does
Phonics Instructions lets podcast creators add phonetic spellings for words that are commonly mispronounced by the AI voice (e.g. brand names like "Evolysse" or "Jeauveux"). Once saved, a phonetic instruction applies globally across all podcasts.

## Who uses it
Podcast creators and content administrators who need to ensure correct pronunciation of brand names, product names, or technical terms in AI-generated podcasts.

## How it works
1. In the podcast Edit Studio, the user clicks the "edit phonics" button in the right side panel (next to the transcript tab).
2. The phonics instruction guide opens on the left side.
3. The user enters the word and its phonetic spelling.
4. The system shows how many instances of the word appear across how many podcasts.
5. The user previews the pronunciation and saves the instruction.
6. The instruction is added to the phonics guide list.
7. All future playback of any podcast will pronounce that word as instructed.
8. The user can delete phonetic instructions at any time from the guide.

## Things to know
- Phonetic instructions apply globally — changing the pronunciation of a word changes it across all podcasts, not just the one being edited. (source: 38c38369-082f-47e8-865c-be38ac9ae87d_Phonics_Instructions.pdf)
- The feature was prioritised specifically because Evolus was experiencing mispronunciation of key product names across their podcasts. (source: 38c38369-082f-47e8-865c-be38ac9ae87d_Phonics_Instructions.pdf)
