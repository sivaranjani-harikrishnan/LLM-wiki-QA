# Feature: Gamification

**Summary**: A planned feature to boost engagement and adoption of the HeySales learning platform through gamification mechanics, targeting both learners and administrators.

**Sources**: `raw/9acdca04-09dd-4427-b2c9-61754aa18566_Gamification.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it does
Gamification introduces game-like mechanics into the HeySales learning platform to increase learner adoption and engagement. The initial intent is to boost adoption within the Evolus account.

## Who uses it
- **Learners**: Experience gamified elements as they progress through learning content.
- **Administrators**: Configure and manage gamification settings.

## How it works
No detailed requirements or acceptance criteria have been defined in the source document. The document records intent and target audiences only. Specific mechanics (points, badges, leaderboards, streaks, etc.) are not yet specified.

## Things to know
- The source document contains only an intent statement and target audience (learners and administrators); the Requirements field is empty. No formal acceptance criteria exist for this feature. (source: 9acdca04-09dd-4427-b2c9-61754aa18566_Gamification.pdf)
- The initial use case is boosting adoption for Evolus internally. (source: 9acdca04-09dd-4427-b2c9-61754aa18566_Gamification.pdf)
- The course completion screen (see [[courses]]) already displays a leaderboard position as part of completion feedback, which may form the foundation of gamification mechanics.
