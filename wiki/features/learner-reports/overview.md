# Feature: Learner Reports

**Summary**: A stage-based rep scorecard system that surfaces the right coaching signal at each stage of a rep's journey — from onboarding through ramping to independent selling — with actionable next steps for managers.

**Sources**: `raw/dd1587b0-9cea-4b90-bf28-83450e64c150_Learner_Reports.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it does
Learner Reports provides a per-rep scorecard that adapts its "north star" metric based on the rep's current stage. The goal is to ensure every insight is paired with a specific next action, so managers know exactly what to do (not just what the score is).

## Who uses it
- **Front-line managers** (primary): need to triage multiple reps and know within 30 seconds whether intervention is needed and what type.
- **Sales reps** (secondary): onboarding reps track readiness; active reps track skill development vs peers.
- **Enablement and L&D teams** (tertiary): aggregate signals to identify curriculum gaps.

## How it works

### Three stages with different north star metrics

| Stage | Rep situation | North star metric |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Onboarding — not yet on calls | Readiness score |
| Stage 2 | Ramping — on calls, early pipeline | Call excellence score |
| Stage 3 | Independent — enough deal history | Win rate |

### Stage 1 — Readiness (two pillars)
- **Pillar 1 — Knowledge**: All required courses passed at 70%+ minimum.
- **Pillar 2 — Simulations**: Three frameworks assessed (discovery, demo/value prop, closing); best score used per framework; minimum 3 attempts per framework required; 85% average required; 70% floor rule (no framework may fall below 70%).

### Stage 2 — Call Excellence
- Composed of 60% call quality score + 40% stage conversion rate (discovery to proposal).
- Uses the same scoring rubric as simulations, enabling a "transfer gap" comparison.
- **Transfer gap**: simulation score minus real call score on the same framework. A gap of 10+ points means the rep has the skill in practice but can't apply it live.

### Stage 3 — Win Rate
- Activates at 8+ closed deals in a rolling 90-day window.
- Calculated as closed won divided by total closed deals (rolling 90-day).
- Shown with sample size for context.
- Call quality becomes the diagnostic layer under win rate.

### Rep self-view
- Reps see the same cards as managers but with different language — directional rather than diagnostic.
- Manager assignment controls are hidden from reps.

## Things to know
- The floor rule means a rep scoring 95% on two frameworks but 65% on one is NOT ready — gaps cannot be masked by excellence elsewhere. (source: dd1587b0-9cea-4b90-bf28-83450e64c150_Learner_Reports.pdf)
- Transfer gap signals a coaching problem (live pressure), not a knowledge problem — more training will not fix it. (source: dd1587b0-9cea-4b90-bf28-83450e64c150_Learner_Reports.pdf)
- "At risk" status for a framework does not revert the rep's stage; it raises a targeted coaching flag. (source: dd1587b0-9cea-4b90-bf28-83450e64c150_Learner_Reports.pdf)
