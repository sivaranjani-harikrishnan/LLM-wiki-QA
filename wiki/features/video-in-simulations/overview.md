# Feature: Video in Simulations

**Summary**: Enables camera-on simulations where the learner sees their own video alongside the AI prospect's image, and the session is recorded for post-completion review.

**Sources**: `raw/a5bf57bb-4b08-4874-bb07-a5d0b37cb20e_Video_in_Simulations.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it does
Video in Simulations lets creators set up simulations in video mode. During the simulation, the learner's camera is on and they can see themselves alongside the AI prospect. The session is recorded and the recording is included in the post-completion report for self-review and manager review.

## Who uses it
Learners (reps) practising sales calls with camera-on feedback, and managers reviewing recordings as part of coaching.

## How it works
1. The simulation creator selects "video" mode when configuring the simulation (vs audio-only mode).
2. When the learner starts the simulation, the system requests camera permission.
3. During the simulation, both the prospect image and the learner's live video feed are shown in parallel.
4. After the learner completes the simulation, the session recording is included in the post-completion report.
5. Managers can view the recording from within the report.

## Things to know
- The creator decides at setup whether the simulation uses video or audio mode — learners cannot override this choice. (source: a5bf57bb-4b08-4874-bb07-a5d0b37cb20e_Video_in_Simulations.pdf)
- Camera permission is requested at the start of each video simulation. (source: a5bf57bb-4b08-4874-bb07-a5d0b37cb20e_Video_in_Simulations.pdf)
