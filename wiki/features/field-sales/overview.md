# Feature: Field Sales

**Summary**: Field Sales is a planned integration combining Plaud/Fireflies call recording with CRM systems to score in-person or field sales calls. The source document is an early-stage research stub.

**Sources**: `84dcb8a0-4813-4f10-9382-0172f7e33364_Field_Sales.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it does

The intent is to extend Real Call Scoring to field sales scenarios by integrating Plaud and Fireflies (physical call recording devices/services) with the CRM. This would allow scoring of calls that are not conducted over Zoom.

## Status

Early-stage research. The source PDF references a YouTube Short and notes the goal to "Record" — no formal ACs or detailed requirements exist yet.

## Things to know

- The source document is titled "Plaud/Fireflies x CRM" and references a YouTube Short. (source: `84dcb8a0-..._Field_Sales.pdf`)
- Plaud and Fireflies are already integrated separately for real call scoring (see [[real-call-scoring]]). Field Sales appears to be an extension of that work for non-Zoom calls.
