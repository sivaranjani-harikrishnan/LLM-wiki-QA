# Feature: Simulation — Foreign Languages

**Summary**: Allows sales reps to practice call simulations in a foreign language, with the AI prospect communicating entirely in the chosen language.

**Sources**: `raw/8593129a-9341-4b00-85f3-c9d04a539c59_Simulation_-_Foreign_Languages.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it does
Foreign Language Simulations enable reps to select a language other than English when setting up a call simulation. The AI prospect conducts the entire conversation in that language, and the rep is expected to respond in the same language.

## Who uses it
Sales reps and managers who need to practice or assess sales conversations in non-English languages (e.g. Spanish, French, German).

## How it works
1. During simulation setup, the creator selects a language from a predefined list.
2. The default is English if no language is selected.
3. The chosen language is displayed in the simulation overview before the call begins and in UI cards across the platform.
4. During the simulation, the AI prospect communicates entirely in the chosen language including the opening greeting.
5. The rep must respond in the same language.
6. If the rep responds in a different language, the prospect continues in the chosen language and does not switch.
7. Performance is evaluated in the chosen language.
8. Reports and feedback are localized to the selected language or appear in English (configurable).

## Things to know
- The AI prospect will not accommodate language switching from the rep — it stays in the chosen language throughout. (source: 8593129a-9341-4b00-85f3-c9d04a539c59_Simulation_-_Foreign_Languages.pdf)
- The selected language is visible on simulation UI cards across the platform, making it easy to identify language-specific simulations. (source: 8593129a-9341-4b00-85f3-c9d04a539c59_Simulation_-_Foreign_Languages.pdf)
